# Roast the Smart Fridge — One Line

**The line (137 characters):**

> My smart fridge films every 3 a.m. cheese raid for the cloud, snitches to my fitness app, then needs a firmware update just to stay cold.

## Why this line

The roast stacks three real smart-fridge behaviours into one escalating betrayal: the interior camera that photographs your food (here, catching a 3 a.m. cheese raid and sending it to the cloud), the app ecosystem that reports your eating habits to your fitness tracker, and the firmware-update treadmill — ending on the sting that a fridge needs a software update to perform its one ancestral job: staying cold. No stock milk-reorder joke; the fridge is the snitch, not the shopper.

## How the program works

`main.py` is a stdlib-only, deterministic, offline Python program. It holds the line as a constant and writes it, byte for byte with no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt` (the output directory can be overridden with `ENTRY_OUTPUT_DIR` for local testing). Run it any number of times and the output is identical, satisfying the gate: UTF-8 text of 1–160 characters, not only whitespace.
